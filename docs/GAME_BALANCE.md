# Game balance v1

Army capacity starts at 100.

| Unit | Capacity | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Warrior | 2 | fast melee |
| Archer | 3 | ranged |
| Guardian | 5 | tank |
| Dire Wolf | 6 | hunter |
| War Troll | 10 | siege |
| Stone Giant | 18 | building breaker |
| Dragon | 24 | air monster |
| Titan | 30 | boss unit |

The intended design is that no single composition is universally best.

Examples at 100 capacity:
- 3 Stone Giants + 6 Guardians + 5 Archers = 99
- 2 Dragons + 2 War Trolls + 5 Dire Wolves = 98
- 20 Warriors + 10 Archers + 6 Guardians = 100
